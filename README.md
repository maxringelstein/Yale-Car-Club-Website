# Yale Car Club — Official Website

The official website for Yale Car Club, a student-run automotive community at Yale University. Built and maintained by the club's leadership.

🌐 **Live site:** [yalecarclub.com](https://www.yalecarclub.com)

## About the Club
Yale Car Club was founded in 2026 to bring together students, alumni, and industry professionals around a shared interest in cars. Membership is free and open to all Yale students, faculty, staff, and alumni.

## Features
- **Upcoming Events** — Registration links for Cars & Coffee and the Yale Car Show
- **Leadership Profiles** — Meet the club's officers
- **Campus Car Stream** — Community-submitted photos of interesting cars spotted on and around Yale's campus
- **Past Events Gallery** — Photos from shows the club has attended
- **Join Page** — Sign-up form linking to the club's mailing list and Slack

## Tech Stack
`HTML` · `CSS` · `JavaScript` · `Cloudinary` (image hosting) · `Vercel` (deployment)
